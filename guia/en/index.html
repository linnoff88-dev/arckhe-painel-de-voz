In short

Voice Panel is an interface for people who use or want to build a personal assistant like Intelecto. It brings together a microphone, editable text, conversation, and a holographic core that shows the assistant's state. In the demo, you can hear a briefing, a spoken summary with fictional data. To talk to your own assistant, install the local bridge, the program that connects the panel to the assistant on your computer. The idea came from Mark-LVII by FatihMakes.

What you get

Speak and review

Check what the microphone understood and correct names or times before sending.

Follow the response

The hologram changes pace while the panel records, processes, or speaks. The conversation keeps the responses from the open tab.

Connect your assistant

Use Intelecto v2 or connect another agent through a bridge. The panel works with whichever engine you choose and adapt.

Where the idea came from

Mark-LVII demonstrates voice, an avatar, briefings, and computer control. The code also includes resumable tasks, quiet hours for routines, and citation checking. The analysis shows what could become a plugin, an extension that adds a feature to the assistant.

The panel in your day

In the morning, try a briefing with fictional reminders. Before a meeting, dictate a question, review the recognized time, and send it. If you prefer silence, turn off the sound and read the response.

How it works

Imagine an intercom with a screen. The microphone receives your question, the screen shows what was said, and a bridge forwards your request to the assistant installed on your computer. On the public page, you can see the panel and hear a sample briefing.

Getting started

  1. Open the demo. Click “Ligar som” and then “Mostrar briefing de exemplo” to hear the fictional summary.
  2. To test your voice, click “Gravar pergunta” and allow microphone access if prompted. If “Baixar português para o microfone” appears, do that and try recording again. Speak, click “Parar e transcrever,” and review the text. If the browser says the feature is incompatible, you can type or use the local installation.
  3. To connect your assistant, follow the installation instructions from the Instalação técnica button. If you already use Codex or Claude Code, you can also download the project, open that folder in the tool, and paste the request below.
